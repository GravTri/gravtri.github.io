GravTri

The Gravity Trident

A multi-scale and multi-messenger approach to testing general relativity through galaxy clusters, cosmic large-scale structure and gravitational waves.

Explore the Science
Research

Three pillars. One test of gravity.

GravTri combines three complementary observational probes to investigate gravitational physics across different spatial scales, connecting the internal structure of galaxy clusters with cosmological structure formation and gravitational-wave lensing.

PILLAR 01

Gravitational Redshifts

Measuring gravitational redshifts in galaxy clusters to probe their gravitational potentials and test deviations from general relativity.

PILLAR 02

Galaxy-Cluster Clustering

Using the spatial distribution and clustering statistics of galaxy clusters to investigate the growth of cosmic structure and constrain modified-gravity models.

PILLAR 03

Gravitational-Wave Lensing

Exploiting the strong gravitational lensing of gravitational waves by galaxy clusters, particularly their time delays, to test gravitational physics.
