The Gravity Trident

A multi-scale and multi-messenger approach to testing general relativity through galaxy clusters.

Explore the Science
Research

Three probes. One framework for testing gravity.

GravTri combines gravitational redshifts, strong lensing of gravitational waves, and clustering as complementary observational probes of gravity across multiple physical scales, all using galaxy clusters.

Pillar I: intracluster scales

Gravitational redshifts

Measuring gravitational redshifts in galaxy clusters to probe the relationship between gravitational potentials and mass and test deviations from general relativity.

Pillar II: cluster scales

Clustering

Using the spatial distribution and clustering statistics of galaxy clusters to investigate the growth of cosmic structure as a function of redshift to constrain departures from general relativity.

Pillar III: intercluster scales

Gravitational-wave lensing

Exploiting the strong gravitational lensing of gravitational waves by galaxy clusters to test gravitational physics.
